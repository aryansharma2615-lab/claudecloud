# Print-farm automation that already exists (FarmHand research)

Written 2026-10-02 for Shawarma Prints. Scope: how existing products and projects clear finished parts and swap plates with nobody home. Target machines: Bambu Lab H2S (enclosed, glass door, 340x320x340 mm) and Creality Ender 3 S1 Pro (open-frame bed-slinger, magnetic spring-steel PEI sheet).

## How to read this (limits of the research)

- **Page fetching was blocked** by the network proxy for every site except GitHub, and the web-search budget ran out (200/200) before I finished. Vendor and press facts below come from search-engine page summaries, not from reading the pages. Treat every number tagged `[web]` as "as summarised, re-check before spending money".
- Items tagged `[repo]` come from GitHub repositories I cloned and read directly (code, README, changelog).
- Grades: **A** = built + documented + video + measured numbers. **B** = built, photos/video or code, few numbers. **C** = vendor claim or spec sheet. **D** = render, forum hearsay, unverified. Nothing here earns an A: I could not watch any video or see measured cycle times.
- Money: all prices are **USD**, seen 2026-10-02 in search summaries (page dates unknown). **No CAD conversion**: I had no exchange-rate source, and I will not invent one.
- Terms: PEI = polyetherimide, the textured coating on build plates. G-code = the printer's command text. End G-code = G-code the slicer appends to a job, run after the last layer. MQTT = the message protocol Bambu printers speak on the local network. AMS = Bambu's Automatic Material System (filament unit). PLA/PETG/ABS/ASA/TPU = filament types. BoM = bill of materials. PCB = printed circuit board.

## Bottom line

- **Two products claim to support the H2S/H2D today**: FarmLoop Stage 1/Stage 2 (3D Farmers) and the AutoFarm3D Door Opener (3DQue). Both keep the plate in the printer, flex or cool it, and push parts off with the toolhead. **Nobody I found swaps plates on an H2S/H2D.** Plate swappers (SwapMod, JobOx) exist for the A1 mini, not the H series. Bambu Lab sells no ejector or plate changer that I could find.
- The only plate-swap-by-robot system with a price is **Prusa Pro AFS** (EUR 45,000 / USD 50,000, nine custom printers), and it needs custom printers.
- No vendor documents a cycle time for the H-series kits. Cool-down to a release temperature (25 to 44 C in the sources) is the dominant, undocumented wait.

## What to take

- **Nothing existing replaces our robot's plate-swap job on the H2S.** Competing H-series kits flex the plate in place and push parts off with the toolhead; that is the field's answer to "clear parts", and it is cheap ($199 to $235 for the kits seen).
- Our flex-and-push alternatives are well-trodden: bed-edge flexing plus a TPU-faced or scraper push-off is the common design on bed-driven printers. A robot gripper that flexes the plate edge and sweeps parts into a bin is the same physics with more reach.
- **Release temperature is the real timer.** Every source gates the clear on a cooled bed: 25 C (SimplyPrint example), 29 C (3DQue PLA), 30 C (InfinityFlow3D), 44 C (PrintFarming P1S), 25 to 35 C (Continuous Print). Poll the real bed temperature instead of trusting a G-code wait.
- Plate-in-place beats plate-out for farms today: the only plate swappers (SwapMod, JobOx) are A1-mini scale mechanical magazines (up to 15 plates, 87 s), and the industrial one (Prusa AFS) is USD 50,000 for custom printers.
- Plate choice is a design input: textured PEI works, SuperTack and Frostbite-style grippy plates break the cycle. Pick one validated plate type per printer and stock only that.
- The Ender 3 S1 Pro is a bed-slinger with a spring-steel sheet: Loop/Chain Production/3DQue-style sweeps on this class are documented; belt conversion is not a fit (adhesion, PETG tearing, slower).
- Vision-based "is the plate empty" gating (Bambuddy Plate Guard: two fresh snapshots, fails closed) is already a pattern; copy it.
- Door automation is considered mandatory for enclosed Bambus (FarmLoop Stage 2 uses 64 N; 3DQue sells a standalone door opener). Our robot's door job is validated.

## Documented failures → rule

| Failure | Source | Rule it creates for our robot |
|---|---|---|
| Toolhead's magnetic front cover is knocked off by the push; firmware faults and drops the bed to its lowest position; needs power cycle + re-home (P/X series; causes: part not released, push too low (old app used 2 mm), first-version pusher) | [FarmLoop docs](https://help.3d-farmers.com/troubleshooting/toolhead-cover-pops-off/) | Never contact the toolhead body; verify detach before any push; any firmware fault after a motion = stop and alert |
| Grippy plates (Cool Plate SuperTack, Frostbite) do not release, or the whole plate shears off the bed during push | [plates](https://help.3d-farmers.com/getting-started/choosing-a-build-plate/) | One approved plate type per printer; hold the plate down during any sideways force |
| Limited detach force: big or strongly stuck prints stay; pushing beyond the sweet spot makes Z skip under load and crush plate against bender | [FarmLoop docs / MakerWorld Stage 1 page](https://makerworld.com/en/models/2829187-farmloop-stage-1-h2s-h2d-h2c-automatic-printing) (exact page not confirmed) | Cap part footprint/adhesion for unattended jobs; force-limit the gripper; stuck part = pause, not retry harder |
| Part not detached because bed too warm | [FarmLoop troubleshooting](https://help.3d-farmers.com/troubleshooting/) | Release temperature = measured manual-release temp minus 4 to 6 C; calibrate per material |
| PETG sticks too hard on release beds; needs +0.1 to 0.2 mm Z offset and hotter first layer | [3DQue docs](https://docs.3dque.com/docs/troubleshooting/vaapr-bed-troubleshooting/) | Start unattended mode with PLA only; PETG gets its own profile after validation |
| "Wait for bed temperature" ignored while cooling on some printers; some P1S firmware exits near 45 C | [Continuous Print](https://github.com/smartin015/continuousprint/blob/master/docs/managed-bed-cooldown.md), [PrintFarming script](https://github.com/jeremyjacob/PrintFarming/blob/main/farm_postprocess.py) | Read bed temperature over the network and decide in our code; add a fixed dwell fallback |
| "Finished" state arrives after end G-code already moved things; camera at finish may show a swapped plate | [Bambuddy source](https://github.com/maziggy/bambuddy) | Treat `FINISH` as "motion may still be running"; confirm bed state by camera after the printer is idle |
